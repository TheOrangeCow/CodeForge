Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 September 2026

1. Who we are

CodeForge (the "Site") at https://codeforge.theorangecow.org is operated by Daniel Foster, an individual based in Southampton, England, trading as TheOrangeCow ("we", "us", "our"). We are responsible for the personal data we process through the Site.

If you have a question about your personal data or this Privacy Policy, contact us at help@theorangecow.org.

2. What information we collect

When you create an account, we collect:

  • your username;
  • your email address;
  • a password hash used to verify your password without storing the password itself; and
  • the date and time your account was created.

When you use CodeForge, we may also store information generated by your activity on the Site, including:

  • problems you submit and information associated with those problems;
  • answers you submit to problems and whether each submission was correct;
  • the date and time of submissions;
  • ratings you give to problems and the date and time of those ratings;
  • reviews, review decisions and comments made as part of the problem review process;
  • book requests, including the requested book name, description and reason; and
  • administrative decisions or notes associated with content you submit.

Your public CodeForge profile may display your username, points, number of solved problems and the problems you have solved.

3. How we use your information

We use this information to:

  • create and maintain your CodeForge account;
  • authenticate you and keep you signed in;
  • record problem attempts and determine whether answers are correct;
  • calculate points, solved-problem counts and leaderboard information;
  • allow users to submit and review coding problems;
  • process book requests;
  • display public user profiles and CodeForge features;
  • operate, maintain and secure the Site;
  • investigate misuse, abuse or security problems; and
  • comply with legal obligations where required.

We do not sell your personal data and we do not use it for targeted advertising.

4. Legal bases for processing

Where UK data protection law applies, we generally rely on the following legal bases:

  • Contract: where processing is necessary to provide your CodeForge account and the features you request.
  • Legitimate interests: where processing is necessary to operate, secure, maintain and improve CodeForge, prevent misuse and protect the Site and its users.
  • Legal obligation: where we need to process or retain information to comply with the law.
  • Consent: where we specifically ask for your consent and rely on it as the legal basis for a particular use.

5. Passwords and security

Your password is not stored as plain text. CodeForge stores a password hash and uses it to verify your password when you sign in.

We take reasonable measures to protect information held by the Site. However, no website or method of storing information can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

You should use a unique password for CodeForge and should not enter passwords, private keys, personal information or other confidential information into problem content or other public submissions.

6. Cookies and sessions

CodeForge uses session information to provide account and authentication functionality, including keeping you signed in and maintaining certain temporary information needed by the registration process.

We do not currently use advertising cookies or third-party analytics cookies.

7. Third-party services

CodeForge is hosted using a third-party hosting provider. The hosting provider may process information necessary to operate the server, including technical information contained in server and security logs. We use the provider as a service provider for hosting the Site.

The Site currently loads fonts from Google Fonts. This can cause your browser to make a request to Google's servers when a page using those fonts is loaded. Google may receive technical information associated with that request, such as your IP address. If we change the Site to serve these fonts locally, this section will be updated.

8. Server and security logs

Our hosting infrastructure may generate technical logs as part of operating and securing the Site. These logs may include information such as IP addresses, request information, timestamps, browser or device information and error information.

We use such information for security, troubleshooting, preventing abuse and maintaining the Site. We do not use server logs for advertising.

We retain logs only for as long as reasonably necessary for these purposes, taking account of security, operational and legal requirements.

9. Who we share information with

We may share or allow access to information with:

  • our hosting and infrastructure providers where necessary to operate CodeForge;
  • service providers acting on our behalf where necessary to provide or maintain the Site; and
  • law enforcement, regulators or other authorities where we are legally required to do so or where disclosure is necessary to protect rights, safety or the Site.

We do not sell personal information to third parties.

10. How long we keep information

We keep account and activity information for as long as it is reasonably necessary to operate your account, provide CodeForge's features, maintain records and meet legal or security requirements.

If you ask us to delete your account, we will delete or anonymise information that we no longer have a legitimate reason to retain, subject to information that we need to keep for legal, security, fraud-prevention or other legitimate purposes.

Backups and technical copies may take additional time to be overwritten or securely deleted.

11. Your rights

Depending on the circumstances and the law that applies to you, you may have rights including:

  • the right to ask for a copy of personal data we hold about you;
  • the right to ask us to correct inaccurate or incomplete information;
  • the right to ask us to delete your personal data;
  • the right to ask us to restrict certain processing;
  • the right to object to certain processing, including processing based on legitimate interests; and
  • the right to receive certain personal data in a portable format where applicable.

These rights are subject to legal conditions and exceptions.

To exercise a right or ask a question about your information, email help@theorangecow.org. Please provide enough information for us to identify your account and understand your request.

12. Complaints

If you are unhappy with how we handle your personal data, please contact us first so that we can try to resolve the issue.

You can also complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office (ICO): ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint.

13. Children

CodeForge is intended for people aged 13 and over. If you are under 18, you should have permission from a parent or guardian before using the Site.

If you believe that a child has provided us with personal information in circumstances where they should not have done so, please contact us at help@theorangecow.org.

14. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the Site, the information we process or the law changes. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page shows when the policy was last changed.
